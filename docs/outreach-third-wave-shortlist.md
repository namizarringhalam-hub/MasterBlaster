## Suggested order

Start with IndieDB, PaperCroft and SlowDen for direct player discovery/feedback.
Consider 80 Level and PC Gamer as individual editorial pitches. Rando.gg follows
a real iframe compatibility check. GameDev.net and Show HN are manual routes
under the requirements above. These priorities are judgments based on audience
and route fit; they are not measured reach predictions.
